// About Me

Who is Dev Yadav?

My name is Dev Yadav, and I'm 17 years old. I'm a self-taught developer on a mission — building my first websites from scratch, just like this one. Every single day, I learn something new and push myself a little further.

But coding is only half of my story. I'm deeply passionate about Artificial Intelligence. I spend time every day understanding how AI works — how it thinks, how it learns, how it can solve real-world problems for real people.

"My mind never stops. I wake up every morning with a new idea. Some days it's a small thought, some days it feels like it could change everything — and I write every single one down."

I believe that consistency + curiosity = success. I don't come from a big city or a fancy college. I just have fire in my eyes and dreams in my heart. That's enough to start. 🔥
